Common questions

Why did my properties disappear at 9:00 AM?

That's the game. Every workday starts fresh at 9:00 AM Eastern with $60,000 and an empty street, so everyone races from the same start. Your lifetime stats, level, best day and profile carry over.

How do I change my investor name or avatar?

Tap your avatar in the top right, then Change. Names come from the game's own word lists, so there's nothing to type.

Who are the other players on the rank page?

Real players show with their investor name and avatar. When there aren't enough real players online, AI rivals fill the empty spots, and they're always marked "AI".

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Everything plays offline. You'll just see the AI rivals instead of real players until you're back online.

Where do the interest rates come from?

Each workday's rate follows the U.S. Treasury 10-year yield plus 3.00 points, so it moves with the real market.

What is the Daily Challenge?

One property a day, the same for every player. You get one try. Your score shows on the rank page, next to other players who played the same deal.

Is this financial advice?

No. Deal Desk is a game. Its money math is simplified for play, and it isn't financial, investment or lending advice.
