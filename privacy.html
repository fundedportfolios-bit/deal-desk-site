Privacy Policy

Deal Desk for iPhone · Published by Raymond Vogel · Effective November 21, 2026

The short version. Deal Desk has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. You never type your name, email or anything else into the game. To show real players on the leaderboards, the app creates an anonymous online account on its own and shares your game results and the investor name and picture you pick from the game's own lists. You can turn that off anytime in the app. That's it.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (your properties, cash, settings and lifetime stats) is saved on your phone. It isn't sent to us, except for the leaderboard results described below.

What the leaderboards use

Online leaderboards are on by default. You control them with Show me on online leaderboards in Profile & settings (tap your avatar, top right).

While it's on and you're online, the app signs in to Google's Firebase service with an anonymous account, created automatically on your phone. It has no email, phone number or real name attached. For each day you play, the app posts one record with:

  • your investor name and avatar, which you choose from the game's fixed lists (stored as numbers, never as text you typed), or none if you haven't picked one;
  • your portfolio value and gain for the day, and how many rounds you played;
  • your Daily Challenge score, if you played it, with the game's rules version and that day's rate;
  • the app's version and the time of the post.

Other players can see your investor name, avatar, portfolio value, when you last played and your Daily Challenge score. Nothing else about you is shown.

These records are stored by Google Firebase in the United States.

Turning it off and deleting your records

When you turn Show me on online leaderboards off, the app deletes the leaderboard records this phone remembers posting (up to its last 40 days of play), then stops posting and stops reading other players' records. You'll see AI rivals only. Turn it back on anytime.

Records aren't deleted automatically. Anything older than what the phone remembers, or posted before you reinstalled the app (a reinstall creates a new anonymous account), stays stored until it's deleted on request. To ask, use Help & support in Profile & settings: the email it starts includes your Player ID (the first 8 characters of your anonymous account ID), so your records can be found and deleted.

If you play offline, nothing is posted and the game works normally.

Crash reports

If the app runs into an error, it sends a technical report to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so we can fix the problem. A report includes things like the app version, the device model, the iOS version and what went wrong in the app. Reports are set up to leave out personal information: no user details, no screenshots and no recording of what you tap.

Updates, settings and rates

The app downloads its own updates (through Expo), a small settings file and each day's interest rate (both from GitHub; the rate follows the U.S. Treasury 10-year yield). Like any website or app download, these services receive standard connection information, such as your IP address and device type, in order to deliver the files.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use them only to answer you. The in-app Help & support link fills in the app's version and your Player ID so we can help faster. You can delete either line before sending.

What Deal Desk does not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of any information.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The game plays sound effects and never records.

Children

Deal Desk is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

  • Turn off Show me on online leaderboards to stop sharing and delete what this phone posted.
  • Play offline to post nothing.
  • Skip choosing an investor name, and the leaderboards show you as "New investor".
  • Email us to delete older records, or with any question about your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Important changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
